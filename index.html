How do I export a WhatsApp chat?
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp.
  2. Android: tap ⋮ → More → Export chat. iPhone: tap the contact name → Export chat.
  3. Choose Without media.
  4. Save or send the file to yourself, then upload it here.
Your chat is read inside this browser. The stats never leave your device. Only if you ask for the AI report is a trimmed, anonymised sample sent to the AI — and nothing is stored.
